**Update (2026):** now published in the *Bulletin of Mathematical Biology*.

This paper applies modern tools, identifiability and sensitivity analysis, to models of how bacterial populations are selected for biofilm formation. The central question: what data would we need to measure the model's parameters accurately?

Using a genetic algorithm to choose sampling times, we found that over a 24-hour experiment, 11 well-chosen time points estimate the biofilm-formation rate more accurately than 51 evenly spaced ones.

The work grew out of a project with Daravuth Cheam, who is running the matching experiments in *Vibrio fischeri* in the Nishiguchi lab.

Williams, S., Cheam, D., Nishiguchi, M. K., Sindi, S. S., Khatri, S., Rutter, E. M. *Bull. Math. Biol.* 88(7), 132 (2026).
