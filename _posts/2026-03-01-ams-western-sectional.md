I was invited to speak at the Spring 2026 Western Sectional Meeting of the American Mathematical Society in Boise, Idaho. The talk covered our recent paper on [symbiotic fluid dynamics in bobtail squid](/updates/thermal-stress-disrupts-symbiotic-fluid-dynamics-in-bobtail-squid/): how the breathing-driven flows that bring bacteria to the squid's light organ change under heat stress.
