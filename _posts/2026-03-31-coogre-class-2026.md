On 31 March 2026 I guest-lectured in COOGRE (QSB 286), UC Merced's workshop-based graduate course run through INSITE. The session had three parts:

1. **What models are for.** The basic ideas of mathematical modeling: what models can tell us, and where their limits are.
2. **A case study.** Brownian motion and bacterial motility, including a short history of how the ideas developed.
3. **Try it yourself.** Participants analyzed data and ran a simple model in a shared notebook.

The worksheet and slides are linked above, so anyone can work through it.
