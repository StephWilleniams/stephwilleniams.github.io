Most of my work on the Hawaiian bobtail squid happens in simulation. In January 2026 I was on Oʻahu for squid collecting and met the animals in person. We worked out of the University of Hawaiʻi's Kewalo Marine Laboratory in Honolulu and collected at Paikō.

## Squidding at Paikō

Bobtail squid spend the day buried in sand and come out to hunt after dark, so squidding is night work: wading the shallows by headlamp, looking for an animal a few centimeters long.
