Last summer, Kyra Ruiz, Antonia Peters and I read Acheson's *Elementary Fluid Dynamics*. This year's book is Randall J. LeVeque's *Finite Difference Methods for Ordinary and Partial Differential Equations: Steady-State and Time-Dependent Problems* (SIAM, 2007): practical, but with enough proof to see why the methods converge.

**What we're reading:** chapters 1, 2, 3, 5, 9 and 11, focusing on numerical methods for PDEs.

**How it works:**

- Discussions are informal: what we did, what was confusing, what was interesting.
- Everyone who comes, me included, picks up homework from the book's exercises, depending on interest.
- Last year we each presented an attempt at one question per meeting. This year the aim is lower pressure: thinking problems through, not getting perfect answers.

Meeting dates are in the reading plan linked above.
