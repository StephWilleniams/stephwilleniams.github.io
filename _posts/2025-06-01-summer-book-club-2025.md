In summer 2024, Kyra Ruiz, Antonia Peters and I read Acheson's *Elementary Fluid Dynamics*. The 2025 book was Randall J. LeVeque's *Finite Difference Methods for Ordinary and Partial Differential Equations: Steady-State and Time-Dependent Problems* (SIAM, 2007): practical, but with enough proof to see why the methods converge.

**What we read:** chapters 1, 2, 3, 5, 9 and 11, focusing on numerical methods for PDEs.

**How it worked:**

- Discussions were informal: what we did, what was confusing, what was interesting.
- Everyone who came, me included, picked up homework from the book's exercises, depending on interest.
- In 2024 we each presented an attempt at one question per meeting. In 2025 the aim was lower pressure: thinking problems through, not getting perfect answers.

The meeting schedule is in the reading plan linked above.
