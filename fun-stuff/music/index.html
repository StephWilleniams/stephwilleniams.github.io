Music

When I'm not working, I spend a lot of my time playing music, mostly saxophone and some flute.

In the Midlands

At the University of Warwick I played saxophone in the Warwick Big Band, Small Band and Wind Orchestra, occasionally the Symphony Orchestra, and a group called Feelin' Saxy. I also played at the Wednesday Night Jazz Jam on Bedford Street in Leamington Spa.

In Merced

While living in Merced, California, I played saxophone and flute with Merced College's Jazz Band (MCJB), which puts on a show in town each term.

Shows I played in:

  • Central California Band Review (guest artists)
  • Fall Concert 2024, Monday Band
  • Fall Concert 2024, Thursday Band
  • Spring Concert 2025, Monday Band
  • Spring Concert 2025, Thursday Band

In Riverside

Since moving to Riverside, California, I have played with the local community jazz combo, swing band and concert band. I'll add recordings here if any of the shows are filmed.
